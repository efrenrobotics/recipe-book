# Arepas de maíz

A crisp shell, a soft middle, and room for whatever is in the fridge.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups precooked white cornmeal for arepas
- 2.5 cups warm water
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

## Steps

1. Stir salt into warm water. Slowly add precooked cornmeal and mix with your hands until no dry pockets remain.
2. Rest for 5 minutes. The dough should be soft and smooth. Add a little water if the edges crack.
3. Divide into 8 balls and flatten into discs about 1.5 cm thick.
4. Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the arepas for 7–10 minutes per side until a crust forms and the centers are cooked through.
5. Rest briefly, split open, and fill with chicken tinga, avocado, or black beans.

## Notes

- Kitchen draft.
- Use precooked cornmeal labeled for arepas, not masa harina.
- Check the cornmeal label for gluten cross-contact if needed.
