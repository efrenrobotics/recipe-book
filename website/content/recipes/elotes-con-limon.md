# Elotes con limón

Sweet corn, a little char, lime, and chile. No mayonnaise needed.

## Ingredients

- 6 ears corn, husks removed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 limes, halved
- 1 tsp ancho chile powder
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

## Steps

1. Heat a grill to medium-high. Brush corn lightly with olive oil.
2. Grill for 12–15 minutes, turning every few minutes, until tender and charred in patches.
3. Squeeze lime over the hot corn. Sprinkle with chile, salt, and cilantro. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Kitchen draft.
- Naturally egg-free. Add cotija at the table if dairy is welcome.
