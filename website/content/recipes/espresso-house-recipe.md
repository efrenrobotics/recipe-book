# The house espresso

A starting ratio, not a rule. Taste, change one variable, and pull again.

## Ingredients

- 18 g freshly ground coffee
- 38 g brewed espresso yield

## Steps

1. Heat the machine and portafilter. Start with a brew temperature of 93°C.
2. Dose 18 g of coffee. Distribute evenly and tamp level.
3. Pull a shot, aiming for 38 g in the cup in about 29 seconds from pump start.
4. Taste. If it is sharp and thin, grind a little finer. If harsh and drying, try a little coarser. Record what changed.

## Notes

- Kitchen draft — these are starting parameters, not a record of an actual shot.
- Beans: medium roast. Dose: 18 g. Yield: 38 g. Time: 29 sec. Temperature: 93°C.
- Grind settings vary by grinder. Adjust by taste.
