# Sopa de Lentejas (Lentil Soup)

A hearty Mexican-style lentil soup with tomatoes, vegetables, and warm spices. Comforting, nutritious, and budget-friendly. Great for meal prep or batch cooking on Sundays.

## Ingredients

- 500 g dry lentils, rinsed and picked over
- 2 medium white onions, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 Roma tomatoes, diced (or 1 cup canned tomatoes)
- 3 medium carrots, peeled and diced
- 3 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 6 cups vegetable or chicken broth
- 2 cups water
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Lime wedges for serving

## Steps

1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add diced onions and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3. Stir in diced tomatoes, cumin, and oregano. Cook for 3-4 minutes until tomatoes break down.
4. Add rinsed lentils, carrots, potatoes, bay leaf, broth, and water. Stir well.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium-low. Cover partially and simmer for 35-40 minutes until lentils and vegetables are tender.
6. Remove bay leaf. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
7. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro and lime wedges.

## Notes

- Brown or green lentils work best — they hold their shape. Red lentils will break down into a creamier texture.
- For a thicker soup, mash some of the lentils and potatoes with the back of a spoon.
- Add spinach or chard in the last 5 minutes of cooking for extra greens.
- Serve with warm corn tortillas or bolillos.
- Keeps well for 5 days refrigerated or freeze portions for up to 3 months.
- Optional toppings: diced avocado, crumbled queso fresco, or a dollop of crema.
