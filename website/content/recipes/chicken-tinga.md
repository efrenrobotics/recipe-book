# Chicken Tinga

A flavorful Mexican shredded chicken in a smoky chipotle-tomato sauce. Perfect for meal prep — use in tacos, tostadas, bowls, quesadillas, chilaquiles, or tortas. Keeps 4 days refrigerated or freeze portions for later.

## Ingredients

- 1.2 kg boneless chicken breast or thigh
- 2 medium white onions, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3-4 chipotle chiles in adobo (from can), chopped
- 2 tbsp adobo sauce from the can
- 400 g canned tomato purée (or 4 medium tomatoes, blended)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 cups chicken broth or water
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- Salt to taste

## Steps

1. In a large pot, heat oil over medium-high heat. Add sliced onions and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3. Stir in chopped chipotles, adobo sauce, tomato purée, oregano, and cumin. Cook for 3-4 minutes.
4. Add whole chicken pieces and broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium-low.
5. Cover and simmer for 30-35 minutes until chicken is cooked through and tender.
6. Remove chicken and shred with two forks. Return shredded chicken to the pot.
7. Simmer uncovered for 10 more minutes to thicken the sauce. Adjust salt to taste.
8. Let cool before storing in portions.

## Notes

- Adjust chipotle amount based on heat preference — start with 2 chiles for mild.
- Chicken thighs give richer flavor and stay more moist.
- Freeze individual portions in containers or bags for quick future meals.
- Serving ideas: over rice with black beans and avocado, in corn tortillas as tacos, on crispy tostadas with cabbage slaw, mixed into quesadillas, or over chilaquiles.
