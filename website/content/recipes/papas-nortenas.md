# Papas norteñas

Crisp edges, soft centers. Potatoes that know their place next to a steak.

## Ingredients

- 1.2 kg baby potatoes, halved
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 220°C / 425°F. Place a sturdy baking sheet inside to warm.
2. Toss potatoes with olive oil, garlic, smoked paprika, and salt.
3. Spread cut-side down on the hot baking sheet. Roast for 35–40 minutes, turning once, until deeply golden and tender.
4. Scatter with parsley and finish with a squeeze of lime. Serve straight away.

## Notes

- Kitchen draft.
- Give them space. Two trays are better than one crowded tray.
- For the grill, use a covered grill and a cast-iron pan over indirect heat.
