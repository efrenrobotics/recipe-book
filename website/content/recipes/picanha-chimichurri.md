# Picaña + chimichurri

A good fire, a generous pinch of salt, and something green. The kind of meal that keeps everyone at the table.

## Ingredients

- 2 kg picanha, fat cap intact
- 2 tbsp coarse sea salt
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 cup chimichurri, to serve
- 2 lemons, halved

## Steps

1. Prepare a grill for two-zone cooking: hot coals on one side, a cooler area on the other. Allow extra time to light charcoal.
2. Pat the beef dry. Lightly score the fat without cutting into the meat. Slice across the grain into steaks about 4 cm thick. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Grill over direct heat, fat-side down first, for 4–5 minutes. Watch for flare-ups and move to the cooler side as needed. Turn and sear the remaining sides.
4. Move to indirect heat and cook to an internal temperature of 63°C / 145°F, checking with a thermometer. Timing depends on thickness and the heat of your grill.
5. Rest for at least 3 minutes; 10 minutes makes slicing easier. Grill the lemon halves cut-side down while the meat rests.
6. Slice against the grain and serve with chimichurri, grilled lemon, and plenty of potatoes.

## Notes

- Kitchen draft — a starting point to cook, adjust, and make your own.
- No cumin. Let the beef and the charcoal do the work.
- Make the chimichurri first so it has time to settle.
