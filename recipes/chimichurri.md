# Chimichurri

A knife, a board, and a lot of parsley. Bright, sharp, and better after a little time.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1 tbsp fresh oregano, chopped
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 tsp sea salt

## Steps

1. Chop the parsley, garlic, and oregano by hand. Keep a little texture.
2. Combine the herbs with vinegar, red pepper flakes, and salt. Stir in the olive oil.
3. Let stand for 20 minutes before serving. Taste and adjust salt and vinegar.

## Notes

- Kitchen draft.
- A good side for picaña. Spoon it on after the beef has rested.
