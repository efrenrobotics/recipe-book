# Frijoles negros con chipotle

Serves: 4 (2 people, 2 days)
Pairs with: chicken tinga, arroz con elote

## Ingredients
- 1 can black beans, with liquid
- ¼ onion, diced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 chipotle in adobo, minced, plus 1 tsp adobo sauce
- ½ tsp Mexican oregano
- Salt, to taste
- Squeeze of lime
- Optional: crumbled feta

## Steps
1. Sauté onion and garlic 3 min.
2. Add beans with liquid, chipotle, adobo, and oregano.
3. Simmer 10 min; mash about a quarter of the beans so it gets creamy.
4. Season with salt and lime; top with feta if using.

## Notes
- No eggs, no cumin.
- GF/DF: skip the feta.
