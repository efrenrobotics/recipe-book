# Vanilla honey latte

A little honey, a little vanilla. A slower start, even on a weekday.

## Ingredients

- 36 g freshly brewed espresso
- 180 ml milk or barista oat milk
- 2 tsp honey
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract

## Steps

1. Stir honey and vanilla into the hot espresso until dissolved.
2. Steam or gently warm the milk, then froth. Pour over the espresso.
3. For an iced version, use cold milk and pour over a glass of ice.

## Notes

- Kitchen draft — adjust sweetness to the beans.
- For dairy-free, use oat milk. Choose certified gluten-free oat milk if needed.
