# Arroz con elote (rice cooker)

Serves: 4 (2 people, 2 days)
Pairs with: chicken tinga

## Ingredients
- 1½ cups white rice, rinsed
- 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained
- ¼ white onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp oil or tallow
- Broth to the rice cooker line (or water + 1 tsp Knorr)
- Juice of ½ lime
- Handful of cilantro, chopped

## Steps
1. Sauté onion and garlic in oil 2–3 min (rice cooker sauté setting or a pan).
2. Add rice and toast 2 min.
3. Add corn and broth; cook on the white rice setting.
4. Rest 10 min, fluff, stir in lime and cilantro.

## Notes
- No eggs, no cumin.
- Already GF/DF if you use a GF broth.
- Optional: stir in a diced roasted poblano or 1 tsp adobo sauce before cooking.
