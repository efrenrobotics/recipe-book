# Steak & Mushroom Rigatoni

Saved from Instagram — post by @_jsproule.

## Ingredients

- Steak (wagyu rump or fillet)
- 1 tbsp butter
- 5-6 sprigs thyme
- 2 cloves garlic
- 220 g rigatoni
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 shallot
- Chestnut mushrooms (2/3 of a pack)
- 150 ml double cream
- 185 ml beef stock
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper
