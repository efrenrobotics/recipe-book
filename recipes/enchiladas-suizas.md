# Enchiladas Suizas

Saved from Instagram — post by @elcomaldediego.

## Ingredients

- Corn tortillas
- Shredded chicken
- Tomato
- Avocado
- Cottage cheese
- Melting cheese
- Crema
- Spinach
- Parsley

## Steps

1. Char 3 halved tomatoes, 2 garlic cloves, half an onion, and half an avocado until blackened in spots.
2. Blend everything with parsley sprigs, spinach leaves, chicharrón de chile serrano, cottage cheese, salt, and olive oil.
3. Optional: thin the sauce with chicken broth — it comes out fairly thick.
4. Warm the tortillas and fill them with shredded chicken.
5. Roll the tortillas into enchiladas.
6. In a warm skillet, lay a bed of sauce, place the enchiladas on top, and cover with more sauce and plenty of cheese.
7. Cover until the cheese melts well.
8. Finish with crema, red onion, parsley, and avocado.

## Notes

- Dietary (gluten-free, dairy-free, no eggs, no cumin) — needs swaps: Cottage cheese + melting cheese + crema → DF alternatives or skip; corn tortillas are GF. No egg or cumin.
- Part of the creator’s "21 formas de cocinar con tortillas" series.
