# High protein creamy lasagna soup

Saved from Instagram — post by @mason_woodruff.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 8 oz sliced mushrooms
- 16 oz 93/7 ground beef
- 1 medium sweet onion, finely diced
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning (no salt added)
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 6 cups chicken bone broth
- 8 oz lasagna noodles (or pasta of choice)
- 15 oz can white beans
- 1 cup whole milk ricotta
- 1/2 oz basil leaves (about 20), thinly sliced
- 2 large lemons, zested and cut into wedges
- Parmesan, for serving

## Steps

1. Heat 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add mushrooms and cook 5-6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until nicely browned.
2. Push mushrooms to one side, add the remaining 1/2 tbsp oil and the ground beef. Cook 2-3 minutes until browned on one side, then break apart and add the diced onion.
3. Cook until the beef is fully cooked and the onions are translucent, another 3-4 minutes.
4. Stir in garlic, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper; cook about 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add some broth to deglaze, scraping up the sticky bits, then add all the broth and the pasta.
6. Cover and cook 10-12 minutes, stirring halfway, until the pasta is just shy of al dente.
7. Meanwhile, blend the white beans and ricotta together until completely smooth.
8. Turn the heat to low and stir in the blended ricotta mixture and a handful of chopped basil. Cover and cook about 5 minutes until back up to temperature and the basil has wilted.
9. Salt to taste. Serve garnished with grated parmesan, lemon zest and juice (don't skip), more fresh basil, and black pepper.

## Notes

- Dietary (gluten-free, dairy-free, no eggs, no cumin) — needs swaps: Lasagna noodles → GF pasta; ricotta + parmesan → DF ricotta-style blend or skip (the bean blend base helps). No egg or cumin.
